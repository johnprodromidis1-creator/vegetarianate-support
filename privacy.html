Vegetarianate the Meal

Privacy policy

Vegetarianate the Meal is operated by John Prodromidis. It converts recipes into vegetarian or vegan versions and calculates prep quantities for kitchen service.

Recipes and saved sheets

Recipe text, dietary selections, portions, and saved sheets are processed on your device and stored in the app. The app has no account, cloud recipe storage, advertising, or analytics. Use Clear device data in the app to remove saved sheets and settings.

Recipe links

When you choose to import a supported recipe link, the app requests that page directly from its publisher. The publisher receives the requested URL and standard connection information, such as your IP address. Its privacy policy applies to that request. Pasted recipes and dietary selections stay on your device.

Sharing and printing

When you share a sheet, the app hands the selected content to your device’s share menu. The app you choose receives that content. Printing uses your device’s print service.

Support

If you contact support, the developer receives your message, email address, and any details you include and uses them to respond. Contact john@thespiceczar.com with privacy questions.
