Vegetarianate the Meal

Vegetarianate the Meal support

Convert a recipe for vegetarian or vegan cooking and plan what to prep for each version.

Import a recipe

Choose Import your recipe. Try a link from a supported publisher or paste a recipe with a title, servings, ingredients, and directions. If a link cannot be read, paste the ingredient list and directions.

Scale and save a prep sheet

Use Mixed service to enter portions for each diet, or One recipe to make a single version. Save the sheet to reopen it on this device. Use Share or Print for a copy.

Review an adaptation

If the app flags a yield or ingredient amount, open Review source quantities to correct it. Some cooking methods, such as baked fillings and egg-set sauces, need a recipe-specific decision.
