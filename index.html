Vegetarianate & Kosherfy support

Adapt a recipe for vegetarian or vegan cooking, plan a meat, dairy or pareve menu, and scale ingredient quantities to your guest count.

Import a recipe

Choose Import your recipe. Try a link from a supported publisher or paste a recipe with a title, servings, ingredients, and directions. If a link cannot be read, paste the ingredient list and directions.

Plan a meal with Kosherfy

Choose a recipe, turn on Kosherfy this meal and select the type of meal you want to serve. For one dish, use One recipe and choose Original, Vegetarian or Vegan as your starting point. A meat plan keeps suitable meat or poultry and looks for dairy to replace. A dairy plan can keep dairy and looks for meat to replace. A pareve plan looks for both meat and dairy to replace.

Review the suggested swaps before shopping. Check the actual packages for the certification and meat, dairy or pareve designation your diners accept. Before cooking, confirm equipment, utensils and service arrangements with the person responsible for kashrut in your kitchen. Kosherfy provides recipe guidance. Certification and Passover suitability require separate verification.

Scale and save a prep sheet

Use Mixed service to enter portions for each diet, or One recipe to make a single version. Save the sheet to reopen it on this device. Use Share or Print for a copy.

Review an adaptation

If an imported recipe needs a correction, open Edit ingredients, yield & directions. Change what needs fixing, then apply your edits to recalculate the sheet.

Shop and compare dishes

Open the shopping checklist for scaled amounts. Use My shopping notes to remember a brand, supplier or item number for an ingredient and dietary selection. Verify the current package before buying. The OU product directory can help you look up a product. To plan a menu, save two dishes, open Check a menu, and select them to compare their targets and portions.
